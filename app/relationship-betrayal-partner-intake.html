Before you begin

This brief form gives me some initial information before we meet. You do not need to give a detailed account of what happened or explain why it happened at this stage. We can explore those things together, at a pace that feels appropriate, once we have met.

Please answer only what you feel able to answer. Some questions are included simply so that I can understand your current circumstances and whether there is anything important I should be aware of before our first session.

This form is not monitored as an emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999 or go to A&E. For urgent mental-health support, call NHS 111 and select the mental-health option.

Confidentiality and no-secrets policy

If I am currently working with you and your partner in couples therapy, please be aware that I work with a no-secrets policy.

Individual sessions and information you share with me are treated with care and respect. However, I cannot agree to keep information secret from your partner where it is significant to the relationship or relevant to our work together as a couple.

This does not necessarily mean that everything you tell me must be shared with your partner immediately. There may be times when we first need to explore how, when and in what way something is communicated. Wherever possible, I will support you to share important information with your partner yourself and in a way that is appropriate and constructive.

If you disclose significant information that you are unwilling to share with your partner, this may affect whether I am able to continue working with you both as a couple.
