Relationship Betrayal – Partner Assessment & Reflection Form

Opening your secure form…

Before you begin

This form helps me understand what has brought you to therapy, the circumstances surrounding the affair or other relationship betrayal, how things are affecting you now, and what support may be most helpful.

Some questions may feel difficult to answer. The purpose is not to judge or shame you, but to begin understanding what happened, what was happening for you at the time, the impact it has had, and any patterns that may be important in helping you move forward.

You can leave a question blank if you do not feel ready to answer it.

This form is not monitored as an emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999 or go to A&E. For urgent mental-health support, call NHS 111 and select the mental-health option.
