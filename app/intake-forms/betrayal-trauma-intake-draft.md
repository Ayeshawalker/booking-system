# Betrayal Trauma Therapy — Initial Information Form

## A note before you begin

Thank you for taking the time to complete this form. It is intended to help me understand what has brought you to therapy, how you are being affected, and what support may be most helpful.

You do not need to describe explicit or intimate details here. Please share only what feels manageable and relevant. You may leave non-essential questions blank and we can explore them gently in a session if appropriate.

This form is not monitored continuously and is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999 or attend A&E. If you need urgent emotional support, contact NHS 111 and select the mental-health option, or call Samaritans on 116 123.
