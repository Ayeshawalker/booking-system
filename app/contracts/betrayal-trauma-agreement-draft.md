# Betrayal Trauma Therapy Agreement

## Our work together

This agreement sets out how we will work together. Please ask about anything that is unclear before signing.

Our work may explore the effects of betrayal on safety, trust, relationships, self-belief and wellbeing. We will work at a pace that supports emotional safety and stabilisation. You will not be required to disclose or explore anything before you feel ready. We will review the focus, pace and usefulness of therapy together.

## Sessions and reviews

Individual sessions are **50 minutes**. You may ask for a review at any time.

Please arrive on time. If you arrive late, the session will still finish at the scheduled time and the agreed fee remains payable.

## Confidentiality and its limits

What you tell me is confidential, but confidentiality is not absolute. If I believe that you or another person is at serious risk of harm, I may need to share relevant information with an appropriate person or service, even without your consent. I may also need to share information where safeguarding responsibilities apply or where disclosure is required by law or a court.

Where it is safe and practical, I will discuss this with you first. I will share only the information needed for the purpose.

I discuss my work in confidential professional supervision. I avoid identifying details wherever possible, and my supervisor is also required to protect confidentiality.

## Cancellation and missed sessions

Please give at least **48 clear hours’ notice** to cancel or rearrange an appointment. A session cancelled or missed with less than 48 hours’ notice **will be charged at the agreed fee**. Genuine emergencies or exceptional circumstances may be considered individually.

If I cancel, no fee will be charged and I will offer another appointment where possible.

## Contact between sessions

WhatsApp or email may be used for appointments, invoices and brief administrative messages. These channels are not monitored continuously and are not suitable for therapy or crisis support. Therapeutic material is best discussed during a booked session.

## Online sessions

For online sessions, please use a private space and a reliable connection where possible. Do not join while driving or doing anything that needs your full attention. If technology interrupts the session, we will try to reconnect and agree what to do.

## Records and recordings

I keep brief, relevant records. My Privacy Notice explains how information is used, protected, retained and deleted. Sessions must not be recorded, photographed, transcribed or processed with an AI tool unless we have both expressly agreed beforehand.

## Emotional safety and additional support

Betrayal-trauma work can bring up strong emotions or memories between sessions. We will discuss ways to support pacing and stability. This service is not crisis support and does not replace medical or specialist services. If another form of support would be safer or more suitable, I will discuss this with you.

If there is immediate danger, call **999** or attend A&E. For urgent medical help, contact **NHS 111**. Samaritans can be contacted on **116 123**.

## Ending therapy

You may end therapy at any time. Where possible, it can be helpful to use a final session to review the work and plan the ending. I may pause or end therapy if it is no longer safe, suitable, within my competence or therapeutically helpful. I will explain this and discuss other support where appropriate.
