# Couples Counselling Agreement

## Our work together

This agreement sets out how we will work together. Please ask about anything that is unclear before signing.

The relationship and the work you agree to undertake together are the focus. I will aim to work fairly with both partners and will not take sides.

## Sessions

Couples sessions are **80 minutes**. Both partners are encouraged to attend consistently. If one person cannot attend, we will agree whether to rearrange, meet individually as part of the couples work, or pause that session.

Sessions are usually booked directly after one another. If you arrive late, we will therefore still need to finish at the agreed time so that the next person is not kept waiting.

## Confidentiality

Confidentiality is very important to me. However, if I feel that there may be a risk of harm to either partner or another person, I may need to share relevant information to help keep people safe.

Wherever possible, I will always endeavour to speak with you first.

## Individual contact and no-secrets policy

Information shared with me individually may be relevant to the couples work. I will not automatically repeat everything to the other partner. However, I cannot agree to hold information that prevents me from working fairly with both of you. I will support the person who shared it to consider how it can be brought into a joint session. If important information cannot be included in the shared work, couples counselling may need to pause or end.

## Cancellation and changes

Please give at least **48 clear hours’ notice** to cancel, rearrange or shorten an appointment. A session cancelled or missed with less than 48 hours’ notice **will need to be charged at the agreed fee**. If an 80-minute appointment is shortened with less than 48 hours’ notice, the agreed 80-minute fee will still need to be charged. Genuine emergencies or exceptional circumstances may be considered individually.

## Contact between sessions

WhatsApp or email may be used for appointments, invoices and brief administrative messages. These channels are not monitored continuously and are not suitable for therapy or crisis support. Therapeutic material is best discussed during a booked session.

## Online sessions

Each person should join from a private space where possible. Do not join while driving or doing anything that needs your full attention. If technology interrupts the session, we will try to reconnect and agree what to do.

## Records and recordings

I keep brief, relevant records. My Privacy Notice explains how information is used, protected, retained and deleted. Sessions must not be recorded, photographed, transcribed or processed with an AI tool unless everyone has expressly agreed beforehand.

## Safety and suitability

Couples counselling may not be suitable where there is coercion, intimidation, violence or an inability to participate freely. I may pause joint sessions, review safety, or recommend other support.

Therapy is not an emergency service. If there is immediate danger, call **999** or attend A&E. For urgent medical help, contact **NHS 111**. Samaritans can be contacted on **116 123**.

## Ending counselling

Either partner may ask to end counselling. Where possible, it can be helpful to use a final session to review the work and plan the ending. I may pause or end the work if it is no longer safe, suitable, within my competence or therapeutically helpful.
