## Welcome and purpose

Thank you for choosing to work with me. This agreement is intended to create a clear,
safe and respectful foundation for our work together. Please ask about anything that
does not feel clear or that you would like us to discuss before signing.

In couples therapy, the relationship and the work you have agreed to undertake together
are my primary focus. I will aim to work fairly with both partners and will not take sides.
Therapy can involve difficult conversations and strong emotions, but it should not involve
intimidation, threats or abuse.

## Confidentiality

What you share in therapy is confidential. I will not ordinarily disclose information to
another person or organisation without your agreement.

There are a small number of exceptional circumstances in which I may need to share limited
information without first obtaining agreement. These include a serious concern about
someone's safety, a safeguarding responsibility, or a clear legal obligation. These
situations are unusual. Wherever it is appropriate and safe to do so, I would aim to talk
with you before taking any action and would share only what is necessary.

As part of responsible practice, I may discuss aspects of our work confidentially in
professional supervision. I protect your privacy and avoid identifying details wherever
possible.

## Individual contact and the no-secrets approach

I work with a **No Secrets Policy** in couples therapy. If an individual session or
conversation takes place as part of the couples work, there is no automatic expectation
that information shared with me will always remain separate from the joint therapy.

This does not mean that everything said individually will simply be repeated to your
partner. If something important to the couples work is shared, I will support you in
thinking about whether, when and how it can be brought into a joint session. My intention
is to approach this sensitively, at an appropriate pace, and in a way that supports honest
and constructive work rather than leaving either person feeling exposed or caught out.

Very occasionally, information that cannot be brought into the shared work may make it
difficult for couples therapy to continue fairly. If that happens, we will discuss the
situation and consider the most appropriate next step together wherever possible.

## Sessions and attendance

Couples sessions are normally 80 minutes. Please arrive on time. If you arrive late, the
session will usually still finish at the scheduled time and the agreed fee remains payable.

Both partners are encouraged to attend consistently. If one person cannot attend, we will
decide whether to rearrange, meet individually as part of the couples work, or pause that
session. This decision will take account of the purpose of the work and what is fair to both
partners.

## Fees, payment and responsibility

Your agreed fee will be confirmed before therapy begins and recorded in your client
record. Unless we agree otherwise in writing, both partners are jointly responsible for
ensuring that the fee is paid, regardless of which partner usually makes the payment.

Payment is due within the period shown on the invoice. Please contact me promptly if a
payment difficulty arises so that we can discuss it respectfully.

### Fee reviews

Fees may be reviewed periodically, usually no more than once in any 12-month period, to
reflect changes in professional costs and the general cost of living. Any proposed
increase will be communicated clearly and with at least four weeks' notice. We will
discuss the change before it takes effect, and your current fee will remain payable until
the notified date.

## Cancellations, missed sessions and changes to session length

Your appointment time is reserved specifically for you. Please provide at least **48 clear
hours' notice** if you need to cancel, reschedule or change the agreed length of a session.

Couples sessions are normally 80 minutes. If an 80-minute session is changed to a
50-minute session with less than 48 hours' notice, the original 80-minute fee remains
payable because the full appointment time has been reserved and may not be available to
offer to another client.

Sessions cancelled or missed with less than 48 hours' notice will normally be charged at
the agreed fee. I recognise that genuine emergencies and exceptional circumstances occur,
and these may be considered compassionately on an individual basis.

If I need to cancel a session, no fee will be charged and I will offer another appointment
where possible.

## Contact between sessions

Email and WhatsApp may be used for practical matters such as appointments, invoices and
brief updates. These channels are not monitored continuously and are not suitable for
urgent or crisis support. Substantial therapeutic material is usually best explored in a
session.

## Online sessions

For online sessions, please use a private space where you can speak freely, a reliable
internet connection where possible, and a suitably charged device. Please minimise
interruptions and do not join while driving or undertaking another activity that requires
your attention.

If technology interrupts a session, we will try to reconnect and may use an agreed backup
method. We will decide together whether any remaining time can reasonably be rearranged.

## Privacy, records and recordings

I keep brief, relevant records to provide the service, manage appointments and meet my
professional responsibilities. Further information about what I keep, the lawful basis,
security, retention and your information rights appears in my Privacy Notice.

I normally retain adult clinical records for seven years after our therapeutic
relationship ends. They are then reviewed and securely deleted unless there is a specific
legal, safeguarding, complaint or insurance reason to retain them for longer. Further
information appears in my Privacy Notice.

Sessions must not be photographed, recorded, transcribed or processed using an AI tool by
any participant unless everyone has given express agreement beforehand.

## Professional and social boundaries

Our relationship is professional. I will not knowingly connect with current clients through
personal social-media accounts. If we meet in public, I will not acknowledge you first, to
protect your privacy; you are free to acknowledge me if you wish.

## Safety and suitability of couples therapy

Couples therapy may not always be the safest or most suitable form of support. If I become
concerned about coercion, intimidation, violence, serious risk, or either partner's ability
to participate freely, I may pause joint sessions, undertake a safety review, recommend
individual specialist support or refer you elsewhere.

Therapy is not an emergency service. If there is immediate danger, call 999 or attend A&E.
For urgent medical help, contact NHS 111. Samaritans can be contacted on 116 123.

## Scope and limits of therapy

Therapy cannot guarantee a particular outcome and does not replace medical, legal,
financial or emergency advice. I will work within my competence and discuss referral or
additional support if another service appears more appropriate.

## Ending therapy

Either partner may ask to discuss ending therapy at any time. Where possible, I recommend
using at least one session to review the work, consider unfinished matters and plan a safe
ending. I may also end or pause the work if it is no longer safe, suitable, within my
competence, or therapeutically helpful; I will explain this sensitively and discuss onward
support where appropriate.
