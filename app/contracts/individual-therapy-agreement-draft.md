# Individual Therapy Agreement

## Welcome and purpose

Thank you for choosing to work with me. This agreement provides a clear, safe and
respectful foundation for our work. Please ask about anything that is unclear or that you
would like to discuss before signing.

Therapy is collaborative. We will agree the focus of the work and review it as needed.
Therapy can involve difficult thoughts, feelings and memories, and outcomes cannot be
guaranteed.

## How I work

My work may draw on counselling, coaching, Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), Rational
Emotive Behaviour Therapy (REBT) and trauma-informed approaches, according to your needs
and what we agree together. This may include identifying patterns in thoughts, emotions
and behaviour; developing more balanced and helpful perspectives; practising strategies
between sessions; and working towards agreed goals.

Where the work concerns betrayal trauma, we will pay particular attention to emotional
safety, stabilisation, pacing, boundaries and the impact of betrayal on trust and wellbeing.
You will not be required to disclose or explore material before you feel ready. Betrayal
trauma is therefore accommodated within this individual agreement rather than requiring a
different contract.

## Confidentiality

What you share in therapy is confidential. I will not ordinarily disclose information to
another person or organisation without your agreement.

I may need to share relevant information when I am concerned about a serious risk of harm,
safeguarding responsibilities apply, disclosure is required by law or a court, or the work
is discussed confidentially in professional supervision. Where it is safe and possible, I
will aim to discuss a proposed disclosure with you first and share no more than necessary.

### Fee reviews

Fees may be reviewed periodically, usually no more than once in any 12-month period, to
reflect changes in professional costs and the general cost of living. Any proposed
increase will be communicated clearly and with at least four weeks' notice. We will
discuss the change before it takes effect, and your current fee will remain payable until
the notified date.

Please arrive on time. If you arrive late, the session will usually still finish at the
scheduled time and the agreed fee remains payable.

We may agree optional exercises, reflections or practical strategies between sessions.
These are intended to support the work rather than to test or judge you, and we can adapt
them if they do not feel suitable.

## Cancellations and missed sessions

Your appointment time is reserved specifically for you. Please provide at least **48 clear
hours' notice** if you need to cancel or reschedule.

Sessions cancelled or missed with less than 48 hours' notice will normally be charged at
the agreed fee. I recognise that genuine emergencies and exceptional circumstances occur,
and these may be considered compassionately on an individual basis.

If I need to cancel a session, no fee will be charged and I will offer another appointment
where possible.

## Contact between sessions

My preferred method for routine communication is WhatsApp. With your agreement, I may use
WhatsApp to arrange or change appointments, send invoices, share therapy documents and
exchange other brief administrative messages. Email or another agreed method is available
if you would prefer not to use WhatsApp.

I do **not** provide therapy, therapeutic advice, clinical guidance or crisis support by
WhatsApp, email or text message. Messages containing therapeutic material will not usually
receive a therapeutic response. I may acknowledge the message and suggest that we discuss
it during a booked session, where there is sufficient time, privacy and context to work
safely. Messages are not monitored continuously.

Please record your communication preference in the electronic-signature section at the
end of this agreement.

## Online sessions

For online sessions, please use a private space, a reliable connection where possible and
a suitably charged device. Please minimise interruptions and do not join while driving or
undertaking an activity requiring your attention. If technology interrupts a session, we
will attempt to reconnect and agree a reasonable next step.

## Readiness and the therapeutic setting

Please do not attend a session while under the influence of alcohol or non-prescribed
drugs. Prescribed medication should be taken in accordance with your clinician's advice.
If I believe that intoxication means a session cannot proceed safely or meaningfully, I may
pause or end the session and the late-cancellation fee may still apply.

Comfortable and informal clothing is entirely welcome. I ask only that you are dressed in
a way that is suitable for a shared professional therapeutic space, so that clear and
respectful boundaries are maintained whether we meet online or in person.

## Privacy, records and recordings

I keep brief, relevant records to provide the service, manage appointments and meet my
professional responsibilities. My Privacy Notice explains what is kept, why, security,
retention and your information rights.

I normally retain adult clinical records for seven years after our therapeutic
relationship ends. They are then reviewed and securely deleted unless there is a specific
legal, safeguarding, complaint or insurance reason to retain them for longer. Further
information appears in my Privacy Notice.

Sessions must not be photographed, recorded, transcribed or processed using an AI tool
unless we have both expressly agreed beforehand.

## Professional and social boundaries

Our relationship is professional. I will not knowingly connect with current clients through
personal social-media accounts. If we meet in public, I will not acknowledge you first, to
protect your privacy; you are free to acknowledge me if you wish.

## Safety, emergencies and suitability

Therapy is not an emergency service. If there is immediate danger, call 999 or attend A&E.
For urgent medical help, contact NHS 111. Samaritans can be contacted on 116 123.

I will work within my competence and may recommend another practitioner or service when it
appears more appropriate. Therapy does not replace medical, legal, financial or emergency
advice.

## Ending therapy

You may choose to end therapy at any time. Where possible, I recommend using at least one
session to review our work and plan the ending. I may pause or end therapy if it is no
longer safe, suitable, within my competence or therapeutically helpful; I will explain this
sensitively and discuss onward support where appropriate.

## Commitment to the process

Therapy can sometimes feel challenging. I ask that we both attend consistently where
possible, communicate openly, treat one another respectfully and raise concerns about the
process so that we can consider them together. You remain free to make your own choices
and to end therapy at any time.
