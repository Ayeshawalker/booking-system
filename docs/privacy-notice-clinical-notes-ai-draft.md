# Privacy notice addition: clinical notes and AI assistance

**Draft for review — not yet published**

I keep brief and relevant notes about our work so that I can provide a consistent,
safe and professional service. These records may include information about mental or
physical health and are therefore treated as particularly sensitive information.

Clinical notes are stored in my access-controlled booking and record system. My system
uses Supabase as a secure cloud database provider. Access is restricted to my private
administrator account.

I may use an optional AI-assisted editing tool to help organise or clarify wording that I
have already written. The tool does not diagnose, make clinical decisions, decide what
treatment a client receives or replace my professional judgement. I review every
suggestion and decide whether it is accurate before anything is saved as a final note.

When I use this feature, the text submitted for improvement is processed through the
OpenAI API. API data is not used to train OpenAI's models by default. The request is made
with storage disabled, although limited content may be retained in security and
abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days under OpenAI's standard API controls. I minimise
names, contact details and unnecessary information about other people before using the
tool wherever reasonably possible.

I use this information to provide and administer the therapeutic service, maintain
appropriate professional records, protect clients and others where necessary, and meet
legal or professional responsibilities. My recorded lawful basis is set out in my internal
data-protection record.

I normally retain adult clinical records for seven years after our therapeutic
relationship ends. At the end of that period they are reviewed and securely deleted,
unless there is a specific legal, safeguarding, complaint or insurance reason to keep
them for longer.

You may ask me about the information I hold, request a copy, or ask for inaccurate factual
information to be corrected. Other data-protection rights may apply depending on the
circumstances. Please contact Ayesha Jane Walker at **ayeshajane67@gmail.com**. You also
have the right to raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's
Office at <https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/>.
